# Homestead AI interface system

Direction: the farm is Sprout Lands pixel art; the panel is a clean white sheet beside it that only shows what you need to decide now. The farm carries the colour and the personality; the panel carries the hierarchy.

- Type: one sans, Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, for all UI text (titles 700 to 800). JetBrains Mono only for file names, the diff and timestamps. The Sprout Lands pixel font only on the map and for farmhand names in the crew list. No serif.
- Palette: white panel, neutral ink greys, --sprout as the only action accent. No cream, parchment or beige surfaces. Reason labels are bold ink words with a Sprout Lands pixel icon; only Irreversible is red.
- No eyebrows, kicker labels, middot chains or right-aligned side meta. Form fields get a plain label; lists get a one-word heading at most.
- One job per screen. Brief: goal, files, energy. Clarify: questions and choices (the assumed default is dashed). Plan: task, farmhand, files. Grow: the open ask leads; confidence sits in its header, reasoning and the reply box live under "Why it's asking". Harvest: the headline, "The crew needed you N times", the announcement, your decisions.
- HUD: Energy and Needed you only. Other counts are in tooltips and the harvest report.
- Finished steps (Brief, Clarify, Plan) are buttons in the stepper and open a read-only review.
- Lists are ruled, never boxed. Ink border plus drop shadow is reserved for things you act on (buttons, the open ask card, the HUD strip).
- Keycaps only on ask options. Other shortcuts live in the Shortcuts sheet.
- Map: no dashed lines; plot labels show only for the selected farmhand's tasks and the task that is asking; hover tooltips give the rest.
