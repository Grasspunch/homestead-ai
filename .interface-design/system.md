# Homestead AI interface system

Direction: a pixel farm you watch, beside a field ledger you decide in. The map stays Sprout Lands pixel art. The panel reads like an almanac ledger: ruled paper, entries separated by rules and space, not cards.

- Type: Alegreya (display: panel titles, ask questions, harvest headline, 700 to 800) and Alegreya Sans (everything else). JetBrains Mono only for file names, the diff and timestamps. The Sprout Lands pixel font only on the map and for farmhand names.
- Labels are sentence case, 14px/700 ink. No small-caps eyebrows, no breadcrumb kickers.
- Data uses lining tabular figures; prose keeps Alegreya's old-style figures.
- Depth: the pixel "ink border + 2–3px drop" belongs to things you act on (buttons, the open ask card, the HUD strip). Lists are ruled with --rule, never boxed.
- Focal points: the open ask card in the panel; "Needed you" in the HUD (inverted ink cell); "The crew needed you N times" in the harvest report.
- Color: --sprout is the only action accent. The five reason colors (cost, risk, irreversible, failure, anomaly) appear only on reason labels and their consequences.
- Keycaps only on the primary action and the ask options. Other shortcuts live in titles and the Shortcuts sheet.
- Farm terms (Energy, Crew, Farmhand) carry their plain meaning in a title tooltip, not an inline subtitle.
